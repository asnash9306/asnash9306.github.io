🌺 What Is This Application?

balinese-calendar-rust is a powerful yet simple tool designed for anyone interested in the rich cultural heritage of Bali. It computes important dates and information from the Balinese calendar system, including:

  • 🗓️ Saka Calendar – The lunar-solar calendar used in Bali
  • 🌿 Pawukon – The 210-day ritual cycle
  • 🔢 Wewaran – Various week systems (1-10 day weeks)
  • 🌙 Sasih – Lunar months
  • ✦ Wariga – Divination and good/bad day determination

This tool is perfect for cultural researchers, astrology enthusiasts, event planners, or anyone curious about Balinese traditions. It uses astronomical calculations for precise day boundaries, ensuring accuracy you can rely on.
